# Rates and fees publication controls

Reviewed against ASIC RG 234 issued 9 June 2026, particularly paragraphs 234.37 to 234.46 and 234.50 to 234.63, and ASIC's National Credit Code comparison rate summary.

Sources:
- https://download.asic.gov.au/media/cvcjdpy5/rg234-published-09-june-2026.pdf
- https://www.asic.gov.au/regulatory-resources/credit/credit-general-conduct-obligations/national-credit-code

Current implementation:
- No verified lender rate offers are advertised. Historical starting-rate fixtures have been removed from the RateOffers component.
- Calculator rates and fees are user assumptions, not lender offers. Zero fees do not mean fee free.
- Lender and product specific pricing, conditional costs, Lumio service fees and relevant remuneration are explained near decisions and tools.
- A dedicated internal Rates, fees & comparisons resource explains the limits of a comparison rate.
- Important qualifications use readable text rather than relying on a reduced footer disclaimer.

Before publishing an actual offer:
1. Obtain current written lender product authority and pricing, including eligibility, rate range or starting-rate conditions, fixed or variable status, all applicable fees, security and payment conditions, validity and approval to advertise.
2. Confirm legal entity, credit licence or representative disclosures and authorisations; the current site does not establish these details.
3. For applicable consumer credit advertising, show the properly calculated lender comparison rate immediately beside the interest rate and repayment, with no less prominence. Display the required example loan amount and term and the statutory comparison-rate warning applicable to the advertisement. Do not use the scenario calculator to invent a regulated comparison rate.
4. Check the overall impression, benefit and risk balance, price qualifications, comparison basis and evidence. A disclaimer must not contradict or attempt to repair a misleading headline.
5. Have the responsible Australian credit compliance adviser or legal reviewer approve the exact advertisement and customer disclosures. Record approval, product evidence, review date and expiry; remove or update expired offers.

This is an implementation checklist, not a legal opinion or certification that the business, every page or a future advertisement complies with all applicable obligations. No claims of ASIC approval are made.
