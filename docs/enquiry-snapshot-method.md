# Anika enquiry snapshot, 26 October 2026

This is a local rules based illustration, not AI inference or lender serviceability. The status is a budget buffer calculation; never present it as approval or a lender policy result. Only the consumer enquiry currently has this sidebar.

Annualise each entered income by 52 weekly, 26 fortnightly, 12 monthly or 1 annually. Each source has a user selectable Taxable/Tax free/Not sure treatment. Salary, self employment and rental income default taxable; FTB A/B default tax free. Other benefits default Not sure. These defaults do not establish tax treatment for a person's circumstances.

2026/27 full year Australian resident marginal brackets: nil to 18,200; 15% to 45,000; 30% to 135,000; 37% to 190,000; 45% above. A flat 2% Medicare allowance is included. Offsets, deductions, Medicare reductions/exemptions, surcharge, HELP calculations and super are omitted. The estimate may overstate tax at low incomes or understate deductions such as HELP. No tax/net-income status is shown for nonresidents, uncertain tax residency or uncertain income tax treatment. Citizenship is not tax residency. The sidebar requires explicit full-year resident confirmation.

Monthly buffer = estimated net annual income / 12 minus other monthly living expenses, rent/household contribution, all entered monthly liability repayments, and proposed amortising repayment at the entered scenario rate. Rate defaults to an illustrative 10%, excluding fees/balloon. The requested amount is treated as the financed amount; do not subtract a deposit again. Mortgage repayments are entered as liabilities, not deducted again as housing. Zero declared liabilities requires a deliberate checkbox. Blank figures do not count as verified zero. Property value/equity is not used as income or offset against repayments.

Colour thresholds are product UX assumptions, not lender rules: negative buffer red Shortfall; buffer from zero to max($200, 10% of estimated monthly net income) amber Tight; above that green Surplus. Text labels accompany colour. Incomplete data stays neutral. Asset totals minus debt balances show a separate net asset position; credit limits are displayed separately and not added to debt balances. No HEM, lender credit-card limit servicing, risk shading or credit report assessment is performed.
