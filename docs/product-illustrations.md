# Lumio product illustration system

Created with the built in image generation tool on 19 October 2026.

Each product has its own illustration in `public/media/product-SLUG-640.webp`
and `public/media/product-SLUG-960.webp`. Product captions and accessible image
descriptions are in `lib/product-presentation.ts`. These are illustrative scenes,
not dealer inventory, customer endorsements or policy documents.

| Product | Scene | Intended message |
| --- | --- | --- |
| Car loans | Electric SUV, charging post and key | Finance your next car |
| Personal loans | Renovated home with solar roof | Finance personal life projects |
| Leisure finance | SUV towing a caravan on a coastal road | Plan the next adventure |
| Novated leasing | Healthcare professional with a car charging at work | A car arranged through your employer |
| Commercial loans | Work ute and excavator | Finance eligible business assets |
| Unsecured commercial loans | Cafe owner, supplies and equipment | Working capital, stock and growth |
| Comprehensive insurance | SUV inside a translucent protection shield | Understand motor vehicle protection |

Shared generation brief: premium editorial 3D illustration, landscape 3:2,
important objects inside the central safe area, pale lavender ground,
pearlescent white with restrained purple and electric blue accents, stylised
miniature objects, soft studio light and contact shadows. One clear product
story. No text, numbers, logos, coins, interface panels or decorative orbit rings.
The per product scenes above were appended as individual generation prompts.

The responsive product cards use contain rather than crop, so key objects remain
visible. Headlines and plain language product descriptions sit outside the image.
The same product illustration is used on its dedicated page to preserve context.
