Updated: 26 October 2026 | Version 2.0

Tell us what went wrong. We will listen, explain the review process and tell you how to escalate.

## 2. How to complain

You can complain verbally or in writing, without charge. Explain what happened, any reference you have, the outcome you want and a safe way to contact you. You do not need to use the word “complaint” or complete a particular form. You may use an authorised representative. Tell us if you need language, accessibility or other assistance; we will discuss reasonable support.

## 3. How we review your complaint

We aim to acknowledge a complaint promptly, generally within one business day or as soon as practicable. We assess the issue fairly, investigate relevant records and seek further information where needed. The final response should explain the outcome and reasons, any remedy, and your escalation rights. For standard financial service complaints the required response is generally within 30 calendar days. Different deadlines or limited exceptions may apply, including certain credit reporting and hardship matters; we will explain any applicable deadline and any lawful delay. An urgent hardship request for an existing loan should also be made directly to the lender.

## 4. Licensee escalation

You may raise relevant issues directly with the nominated licensee, Finsure Finance and Insurance Pty Ltd: complaints@finsure.com.au; 1300 346 787, option 3; Compliance, Level 27, 10 Carrington Street, Sydney NSW 2000. The current Unified Loans authorisation and AFCA member details are being confirmed for the final Credit Guide. We will identify the responsible entity rather than treat Lumio, Unified Loans and Finsure as the same legal person.

## 5. Independent financial complaint review

If you are dissatisfied or an applicable response deadline has passed, you may contact the Australian Financial Complaints Authority, subject to its rules and jurisdiction: 1800 931 678; info@afca.org.au; www.afca.org.au; GPO Box 3, Melbourne VIC 3001. AFCA provides free independent review of eligible complaints. You do not have to complete every internal step before contacting AFCA for information. Do not delay seeking help because a website or dashboard is unavailable.

## 6. Privacy complaints

Contact compliance@lumiofinance.com.au. Allow us a reasonable opportunity to respond, generally 30 days. If unresolved, you may complain to the Office of the Australian Information Commissioner at www.oaic.gov.au or telephone 1300 363 992, subject to its process. Financial and privacy complaint rights can overlap. Nothing here prevents contacting a regulator, obtaining advice or using another lawful remedy.
