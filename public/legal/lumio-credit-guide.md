## 5. Fees you may pay

A lender or third party may charge application, establishment, account, valuation, settlement, discharge, early repayment or other disclosed costs. Security and guarantee costs can also arise. Review the actual contract and written quote; fees differ by lender and product.

A fee for our assistance, if proposed, must be explained in an appropriate written credit quote or other lawful agreement before the service or charge requiring it. Do not assume our service is free, that the fee is a fixed amount, or that any particular fee will apply until the actual arrangement is disclosed and agreed. The final approved guide must state the actual fee policy.

## 6. Commissions, referral payments and conflicts

Lenders may pay upfront and ongoing commission. Finsure or other authorised participants may receive or retain a share under the applicable arrangements, and a disclosed referral partner may receive a referral benefit. The final guide and required proposal disclosure must explain relevant direct and indirect remuneration, including fees payable by you, amounts or reasonable estimates and other detail required by law. Ask the analyst to explain any conflict and why an option is proposed. A lower interest rate alone does not prove the lowest total cost. These documents do not impose a commission clawback reimbursement on you.

## 7. Responsible lending and your assessment

Where consumer responsible lending obligations apply to our assistance, we must make reasonable inquiries about your requirements, objectives and financial situation, take reasonable steps to verify the relevant financial situation and assess whether the proposed credit is not unsuitable. We must not provide assistance prohibited by those obligations. Where mortgage broker best interests and conflict priority duties apply, those duties also apply; they are not displaced by technology or remuneration. Commercial credit may fall outside parts of the consumer credit regime, depending on the facts. Do not sign an inaccurate business purpose declaration.

## 8. Requesting a written assessment

Where the law gives you a right to a copy of our preliminary written assessment, you may request it without charge, generally within seven years after the assistance. It must be provided within the applicable statutory timeframe; a request before entry into the contract may require provision before the contract, and later requests generally have seven or 21 business day timeframes depending on timing. Contact compliance@lumiofinance.com.au. A calculator result or enquiry summary is not that assessment.
