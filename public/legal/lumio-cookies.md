## 3. Optional analytics

Optional first party analytics are off until you enable them through Privacy choices. The implemented events contain approved page paths and fixed action labels. The controls exclude application, dashboard, login and account paths, and the server rejects sensitive or unapproved fields. Enquiry answers, names, contact details, financial figures and query strings are not sent as those analytics event fields. No third party advertising pixel is currently installed.

## 4. Your controls

Use Privacy choices to keep analytics off, enable them or withdraw your optional choice. The site’s implemented analytics respect Do Not Track. Changing the choice affects future optional events; it does not automatically erase a lawfully held enquiry or essential security records. Your browser may also offer storage controls, which can affect site preferences.
