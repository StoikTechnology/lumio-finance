# Lumio Finance | Privacy Policy

Updated: 26 October 2026 | Version 2.0

How information is handled for Lumio enquiries and credit assistance delivered through Unified Loans.

This policy distinguishes the enquiry website available now from credit services that may follow. ## 2. Information collected now

Consumer enquiries can include name, phone, email, loan product and purpose, requested amount and term, relationship and enquiry type, employment, gross income sources and frequency, living arrangement, citizenship or residency, living expenses, assets and liabilities. Car enquiries can include vehicle condition, seller type, deposit, trade in registration, state and estimated value. The optional live financial snapshot uses tax residency and income tax treatment selections, housing contributions and itemised liability figures. It calculates in the browser; the entered enquiry fields are stored if you choose to submit, while the editable sidebar example rate is not part of the enquiry record.

Commercial enquiries can include business name and website, structure, ABN, ACN, trust and trustee details, directors’ or authorised contacts’ names and roles, turnover, expenses, assets, liabilities and contact details. We also hold consent confirmations and the enquiry reference. General, professional partner and community forms collect their labelled contact, organisation and proposal information. Do not include children’s personal information in community proposals.

Technical infrastructure processes requests, browser and network information needed to deliver and protect the service. If optional analytics are enabled, the site records only approved page paths and action labels; form answers, names, contact details and loan amounts are excluded from those analytics events.

## 3. How information is collected

We collect information you enter and submit, communications with our team and necessary technical information from your use of the site. Where you provide another person’s information, have authority and tell them about this policy. We may need to collect directly from them before providing services. Calculators process scenario inputs in the browser; entering the same figures in an enquiry sends them to us.

## 4. Purpose and consequences of not providing information

We use enquiry information to understand your request, arrange analyst contact, identify what additional information is needed, maintain enquiry records, address complaints and protect the service. Required fields are needed to process this form; if you prefer not to provide them online, contact us to discuss an alternative. Optional information can be omitted. Enquiry details are stored in the site database after successful submission.

## 5. Information and permissions for later credit services

If you proceed, Unified Loans and the relevant representative may need further information about identity, finances, business affairs, credit history, guarantees and supporting evidence. We will explain the purpose, recipients, relevant legal requirements and consequences at that time. Credit reports, identity verification, open banking, lender submission and electronic signatures require the appropriate notice, authority or consent; an enquiry checkbox is not blanket approval. Do not send government identifiers or financial documents through the present form.

## 6. Sharing between Lumio, Unified Loans and Finsure

Information is made available to authorised personnel dealing with your Lumio enquiry through Unified Loans. Where relevant and lawful, information may be provided to Finsure for authorised credit assistance, compliance oversight, complaints, recordkeeping and licensee obligations. We should explain when an enquiry is progressing to such handling and provide applicable notices.

Finsure Finance and Insurance Pty Ltd identifies its own information handling in its privacy policy at www.finsure.com.au/privacy-policy/. The fact that entities work together does not authorise unrelated marketing or unlimited reuse.

## 7. Other recipients and technology providers

Where necessary and lawfully authorised, recipients may include a selected lender, professional adviser you appoint, approved referral partner, verification provider, contractor, insurer or service provider, and regulators, courts or dispute resolution bodies. Current infrastructure includes Cloudflare hosting and database services, the ChatGPT Sites access layer and Google Fonts. Stoik Technology provides or develops the AION, Anika and related technology described on the site. Access must be limited to the actual service and authorised purpose; not all planned integrations are connected.

## 8. Anika AI, improvement and model training

The current forms do not run a live lender matching or generative AI assessment. Anika’s receipt message is a scripted confirmation after storage, and calculator explanations use rules. We do not treat your enquiry consent as permission to train a general purpose AI model on identifiable personal, credit or sensitive information.

Any proposed use or disclosure to Stoik for identifiable model training or a materially different secondary purpose requires a separately assessed lawful basis, clear notice and consent where required. Necessary service processing is distinct from optional training. Deidentified data may only be used for improvement after assessing reidentification risk and safeguards; removing a name alone is not sufficient. Do not assume a related company or a “business improvement” purpose removes privacy obligations.

## 9. Sensitive information, credit information and identifiers

We do not request health diagnoses, biometric templates, racial or ethnic background, government identity numbers or credit reports in these enquiry forms. A medical loan purpose or a pension category can reveal more than you intend, so avoid unnecessary detail. Sensitive information may only be collected and handled on an applicable lawful basis, including consent where required. Later credit information handling must be explained in the relevant credit reporting notice; this policy does not authorise a credit check.

## 10. Overseas processing and storage

Hosting, access infrastructure, support and their subprocessors may involve Australian or overseas processing. A complete current list of processing countries and service provider arrangements has not yet been confirmed for the planned full platform; we do not promise that all information stays in Australia. Contact the privacy officer before submitting if you need country or provider details. Before adding a new overseas disclosure we must identify the relevant recipients and locations where practicable, assess applicable safeguards and update notices. This policy does not seek a waiver of Australian Privacy Principle 8 protections.

## 11. Security and data incidents

We use hosting security features and application controls appropriate to the current service, including restricted server APIs, input validation, same origin checks, consent controls and separation of legacy user records. Access must be limited to people who need information for their role. No online system is risk free. Do not rely on statements about planned controls as certification of implemented security. Suspected incidents should be reported to compliance@lumiofinance.com.au. We assess and respond to incidents and make notifications when legally required.

## 12. Retention and disposal

Enquiries and related communications are retained while reasonably needed for the enquiry, service, complaints, legal obligations or a lawful recordkeeping requirement. Credit assistance records may have specific retention requirements; a blanket period does not apply to every website visitor or data category. The current enquiry service does not provide an automatic deletion schedule or self service dashboard deletion. Request review or deletion through the privacy contact. Information no longer needed must be securely destroyed or deidentified where required, subject to lawful retention and proportionate backup management.

## 13. Access, correction and deletion requests

Contact compliance@lumiofinance.com.au or 07 3523 6347 to request access, correction, information about use or disclosure, or deletion where available. We may verify identity and authority without requesting excessive information. We respond within a reasonable period and explain any lawful refusal and available review options. We do not charge for making an access or correction request; any permitted access cost must be reasonable and explained in advance. A deletion request is not an automatic right to remove records we must lawfully retain.

## 14. Marketing, cookies and choices

The service contact consent is separate from optional marketing. If we send permitted marketing, a simple opt out must be available. Optional website analytics are off unless you enable them and can be changed using Privacy choices. The site respects its implemented Do Not Track preference. See the Cookie and Analytics Notice for the present implementation. You do not have to accept optional analytics to make an enquiry.
